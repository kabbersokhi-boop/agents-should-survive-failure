Agents Should Survive Failure — supplier approval and recovery

0:00 — A new supplier. A human decision.
This project helps a company review and approve new suppliers. AI supports the review. A person makes the decision.

0:09 — Review evidence. Make the decision.
Here is one fictional supplier, Orion Payments. The reviewer checks policy evidence before deciding whether to approve it.

0:18 — Approve once. Save the decision.
The reviewer approves this request. The system saves the approval, the supplier record, and a local notification.

0:27 — The records are saved. What if execution stops now?
The records are saved, but the workflow has not confirmed completion. A restart could repeat work unless recovery handles this safely.

0:37 — Stop the process. Keep the decision.
Now the demo forcibly stops the worker: the process carrying out the task. The saved records remain in the database.

0:46 — Restart. Resume the work.
A replacement process picks up the same workflow. Temporal, the workflow engine, retries the interrupted step without asking for approval again.

0:57 — Attempt two. Work completed.
Here is the evidence: Temporal shows attempt two, followed by completed. The interrupted workflow has recovered.

1:06 — One approval. One supplier. No duplicate records.
Now check the business records: one approval, one supplier, and one local notification. The retry created no duplicates for this case.

1:16 — Why recovery works here.
Temporal remembers the workflow's progress. PostgreSQL prevents the same business action from being saved twice. AI advice cannot approve a supplier.

1:28 — Try the workflow locally.
Try it yourself: review a supplier, approve it, and reproduce the crash locally. The default demonstration needs no paid model credentials.
